# ERC-3643 Coverage Map

This document maps atomic official requirements to the workflow stages that inspect
and adjudicate them. The authoritative primary source is
[ERC-3643](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-3643). ERC-20, ERC-173 and referenced
official interfaces apply only where ERC-3643 relies on them.

Every candidate produced by a focused reviewer is ultimately subject to Depth 3,
`Adjudicate and Deduplicate Proven Deviations`.

Suggested status values: `mapped`, `partially mapped`, `not mapped` and `not
applicable`.
